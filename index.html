Welcome to Integrative Dentistry

Seattle's Best In Holistic Oral Health

We provide a unique perspective and philosophy toward dentistry and your overall health. Our holistic dentists practice dentistry from a naturopathic standpoint, carefully assessing your oral health as a representation of your total health and wellness. We believe in providing our patients with only metal-free, mercury-free, BPA-free, and fluoride-free alternatives to today's more traditional dental treatments. With Integrative Dentistry, our goal is to make sure your whole family enjoys a beautiful smile backed by TRUE WELLNESS.



To achieve this natural balance, our top Seattle doctors blend advanced dental science with holistic therapies in a soothing, eco-friendly environment. We elevate state-of-the-art treatments—like veneers, dental implants, and safe mercury removal—by accounting for nutrition, acupuncture, massage, and homeopathy as direct means of support to your body's natural healing process.

Backed by over 60 years of combined experience, holistic pioneers Dr. Bramley Birrer, Dr. Mitch Marder, and Dr. Paul Rubin have completely redefined gentle, green dentistry. If you are ready for a healthier, more balanced approach to your smile, request your personal consultation with Dr. Birrer, Dr. Christine Cao, or Dr. David Do today.
